Local Emergency Services Directory

This section provides quick access to essential emergency services. Each listing includes a brief description and allows users to call directly by tapping on the contact number in the app.

---

1. Police

For immediate police assistance in emergencies such as theft, violence, or public disturbances.
→ Call Now: 100

2. Fire Department

For emergencies involving fire hazards, including house fires, electric fires, and chemical fires.
→ Call Now: 101

3. Ambulance

For medical emergencies requiring urgent transportation to a hospital.
→ Call Now: 102

4. Poison Control

For expert advice in case of accidental poisoning or ingestion of harmful substances.
→ Call Now: 1800-11-6117

5. Mental Health Helpline

For mental health support, including anxiety, depression, or emotional crisis.
→ Call Now: 9152987821

6. Women’s Helpline

For support in situations of abuse, harassment, or violence against women.
→ Call Now: 1091

7. Child Helpline

For emergency assistance or protection of children in distress.
→ Call Now: 1098

---

Note:
Each number can be dialed directly from within the SOS app using the integrated **tap-to-call feature**. Please only use these numbers for genuine emergencies.
