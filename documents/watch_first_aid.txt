Watch First Aid – Training Videos & Safety Tips

This guide provides access to short, essential first aid training videos with accompanying safety tips. These are grouped under three main categories: Natural Disasters, Medical Emergencies, and Personal Safety.

---

1. Natural Disasters

Learn how to respond safely and calmly during various types of natural disasters. Each video includes what to do before, during, and after the event.

- Earthquakes:
  → How to Drop, Cover, and Hold.
  → What to avoid during a quake (windows, elevators, etc.).
  → Tips for creating an earthquake emergency kit.

- Floods:
  → How to evacuate safely.
  → What to do if trapped in rising water.
  → Electrical safety during floods.

- Fires:
  → How to escape from house fires.
  → Using a fire extinguisher.
  → What to do if clothes catch fire (Stop, Drop, Roll).

---

2. Medical Emergencies

Quick, life-saving actions you can take in case of a sudden medical situation.

- CPR:
  → Step-by-step adult and child CPR.
  → When and how to use an AED (Automated External Defibrillator).

- Choking:
  → How to perform the Heimlich maneuver.
  → Recognizing signs of choking in adults and children.

- Bleeding:
  → How to stop severe bleeding using pressure and elevation.
  → When to apply a tourniquet.

---

3. Personal Safety

Basic actions to protect yourself and others in emergency or threatening situations.

- Robbery:
  → What to do during a robbery.
  → How to stay safe without escalating the situation.

- Self-defense Techniques:
  → Simple self-defense moves for personal safety.
  → Safety tips for walking alone or in risky areas.

---

Note:
Each topic includes a **short, focused training video** followed by **important safety tips** for easy learning and quick recall during real-life emergencies.

Users are encouraged to **watch the videos regularly** to stay familiar with procedures and build confidence in handling emergencies.

---
