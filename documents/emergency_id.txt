Emergency ID

The Emergency ID feature allows responders or helpers to quickly access your vital personal and medical information in case you're unconscious or unable to communicate.

Details shown:

- Full Name
- Blood Group
- Emergency Contact Number
- Phone Number
- Medical History

Purpose:
This digital ID is designed to save time in critical moments by showing essential details on a single screen. Ensure the information is always up to date for accuracy during emergencies.
