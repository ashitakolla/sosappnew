Family Help

The Family Help section allows you to manage critical health and contact information for your loved ones.

You can:

1. Add Contact
   - Add family members who need emergency care tracking.
   - Includes name and phone number.

2. Add Medication
   - Track daily medications for your family members.
   - Store details like medication name, dosage, and time.

Purpose:
This module ensures that each family member’s emergency needs are accounted for — from medications to emergency outreach.
