Emergency Guide

This guide covers essential steps to take during various emergency situations. Each section provides clear instructions that can be followed quickly and effectively during a crisis.

---

Medical Emergencies

Heart Attack:
- Call emergency services immediately (e.g., 911).
- Help the person sit down and stay calm.
- Loosen any tight clothing.
- If unconscious and not breathing, begin CPR immediately.

Choking:
- Encourage the person to cough forcefully.
- If they cannot breathe or speak, perform the Heimlich maneuver.
- For infants, give 5 back blows followed by 5 chest thrusts.

Allergic Reactions (Anaphylaxis):
- Administer an epinephrine auto-injector if available.
- Call emergency services right away.
- Help the person lie down with legs elevated unless they are vomiting or struggling to breathe.

Stroke:
- Use the FAST method: Face drooping, Arm weakness, Speech difficulty, Time to call emergency services.
- Note the time symptoms began and keep the person calm and still.

Severe Bleeding:
- Apply direct pressure with a clean cloth or bandage.
- Elevate the injured area above the heart level if possible.
- Do not remove the cloth if soaked—add another layer.

Burns:
- For minor burns: Cool with water for 10-15 minutes, do not use ice.
- For severe burns: Cover with a clean, non-stick cloth and call emergency services.
- Do not pop blisters or apply creams to deep burns.

Asthma Attack:
- Help the person use their inhaler.
- Encourage slow, deep breathing.
- Call emergency services if there is no improvement after the inhaler.

Diabetic Emergencies:
- For low blood sugar: Give a sugary drink or snack.
- For high blood sugar: Help the person take insulin if they have it.
- If unconscious, do not give food or drink; call emergency services.

Seizures:
- Move objects away to prevent injury.
- Do not restrain or put anything in the person's mouth.
- After the seizure, place them on their side and check for breathing.

Fractures (Broken Bones):
- Immobilize the area with a splint or padding.
- Do not try to realign the bone.
- Apply ice to reduce swelling and seek medical help.

Poisoning:
- Do not induce vomiting unless directed by poison control.
- Try to identify the substance taken.
- Call poison control or emergency services immediately.

Natural Disasters

Earthquake:
- Drop, Cover, and Hold On.
- Stay away from windows and heavy objects.
- After shaking stops, evacuate if the building is unsafe.

Flood:
- Move to higher ground.
- Avoid walking or driving through floodwaters.
- Stay informed through weather alerts.

Tornado:
- Seek shelter in a basement or interior room without windows.
- Protect your head and neck.
- If outdoors, lie flat in a ditch or low area.

Hurricane:
- Evacuate if advised.
- Secure your home and gather emergency supplies.
- Stay indoors and away from windows.

Wildfire:
- Follow evacuation orders immediately.
- Cover your nose and mouth with a cloth to avoid inhaling smoke.
- Use fire-resistant clothing if possible.

Volcanic Eruption:
- Evacuate early if there are warnings.
- Stay indoors with windows and doors closed.
- Wear a mask and goggles to protect from ash.

Landslides:
- Move away from the path of the slide.
- Listen for unusual sounds like cracking trees or rocks.
- Stay alert after heavy rain or earthquakes.

---

Fire Emergencies

House Fire:
- Get out immediately and call emergency services.
- Stay low under smoke to avoid inhalation.
- Do not re-enter the building for any reason.

Electrical Fire:
- Do NOT use water.
- Use a Class C fire extinguisher if trained.
- Turn off the main power supply if safe.

Grease Fire:
- Never use water.
- Cover the pan with a metal lid to smother flames.
- Use baking soda or a fire extinguisher (Class B) if needed.

Wildfire:
- Follow official evacuation orders.
- Keep emergency kits ready.
- Maintain a defensible space around your home.

Chemical Fire:
- Evacuate the area immediately.
- Do not inhale fumes—cover your mouth and nose.
- Notify emergency services about the chemicals involved.
